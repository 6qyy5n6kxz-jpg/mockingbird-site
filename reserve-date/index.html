Reserving your date has moved

You no longer pay a deposit on this page. Here’s how booking a private event works now.

  1. Plan and request your date. Use our planner to choose food, drinks and extras, see a preliminary estimate, and pick a date that’s open on the calendar.
  2. We confirm the details. We’ll reply to check availability, pricing and anything else your event needs.
  3. Sign and pay online. Once your date is confirmed, we’ll email you a private link to your event portal. That’s where you review and sign your agreement and pay your deposit to hold the date.
