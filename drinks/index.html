Wine

If you like sweet white wine…

Try D’Altieri Moscato: sweet, lightly fizzy, with peach and floral notes. Like it pink? Innocent Bystander Pink Moscato is sweet and spritzy, with strawberry and rose. For a little less sweet, Frisk Prickly Riesling is off-dry, with lime and green apple.

If you like sweet reds…

Try Voulet Malvasia d’Asti: sweet and lightly fizzy, with strawberry and cherry. For a less-sweet next step, OPR Old Patch Red is off-dry and jammy.

If you like dry, crisp whites…

Try Cantina Colli Euganei Pinot Grigio for pear and citrus. Want more zing? Matthew Fritz Sauvignon Blanc brings grapefruit and lime.

If you like big, bold reds…

Try Heron Cabernet Sauvignon for black cherry and cocoa. Ghostrunner Ungrafted Red is full, with dark berry and cocoa. Like a little spice? Domaine de Servans Côtes du Rhône is savory, with blackberry and pepper.

Beer, seltzers & more

If you like light beer like Bud Light…

Try Juniper Low Rider Light Lager on tap (it counts toward a flight). Garage Beer is on tap too. Prefer a can? Grab Cincy Light. Garage Beer Lime adds a squeeze of lime.

If you like IPAs…

Try a can of Bell’s Two Hearted, a classic American IPA. Like it juicier? Masthead Hazy Headlines is hazy, with tropical fruit and citrus. Flying Monkey Juicy Ass goes bold, with mango and pineapple. Skipping alcohol? Try Samuel Adams Non-Alcoholic Hazy IPA.

If you like porters…

Try Żywiec Porter for chocolate, caramel and smooth roast. DuClaw Sweet Baby Jesus brings chocolate and peanut butter. In season on tap: Heart State Gourdeous Vanilla Pumpkin Porter.

If you like seltzers like White Claw…

We have White Claw Black Cherry. Want something new? Lone River Ranch Water is a tequila seltzer with lime and agave. Skipping alcohol? There’s White Claw Zero Proof Black Cherry and more Zero Proof flavors.

If you like sweet, fruity drinks…

Try Peach Mango Sangria or Cucumber Lime Sangria on tap. Ace Pineapple Cider is on tap too. In a can, Dragon Heart is lightly sweet, with cherry and raspberry. Heart of Glass Vodka Lemonade is a strawberry vodka lemonade.
