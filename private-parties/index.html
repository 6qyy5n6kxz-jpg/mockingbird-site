Private Event Reservations

A $250 reservation fee is required for private events, with a $100 deposit due to secure your date and time. The remaining balance is applied to your final event total.

Through our partnership with Frank Creations LLC, you can add entertainment, atmosphere, and interactive experiences without coordinating several separate vendors. Choose from the options below or tell us what you have in mind.

Indoor & Outdoor Party Audio

Looking for more of a true party atmosphere? Add a professional line-array speaker outside while an approved or custom playlist plays throughout both the indoor and outdoor gathering areas.

Live Music

Bring live music into your celebration with The Mockingbird’s house band, A Change of Plans. Their familiar mix of acoustic guitar, piano, and vocals can help create the right atmosphere for dinner, drinks, and special moments. Have another musician in mind? We can help coordinate with your preferred performer, subject to venue approval and production requirements.

Optional enhancement

Wine & Canvas Activity

Turn part of your event into a creative shared experience with Wine & Canvas Toledo. Guests can enjoy a guided painting activity designed around your group, occasion, and available event time.
