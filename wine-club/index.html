Wine Club

The Mockingbird Wine Club

The Mockingbird Wine Club is a quarterly wine experience designed for guests who love discovering great wines - without the noise, pressure, or shipping boxes.

Each quarter, members receive a curated selection of wines from around the world plus an exclusive guided tasting at The Mockingbird led by our wine expert.

This is not a bargain club. It's an invitation to drink better, learn more, and enjoy wine in good company.

How it works

Quarterly pickups, guided tastings, local community

Wine Club pickups happen four times per year

Members choose their preference: Red, White, or Mixed

All wines are picked up at The Mockingbird

Each pickup includes a members-only guided tasting event

Wines are selected by our team from the same global producers we already feature

No shipping. No monthly commitments. Just great wine and a great experience.

Quarterly tasting experience

A guided tasting, every pickup

Each Wine Club pickup is paired with a members-only tasting event at The Mockingbird.

  • Guided by our wine expert
  • Focused on the wines in your club selection
  • Designed to be educational, relaxed, and approachable
  • Multiple time slots offered as needed

Guests are welcome for an additional fee.

This tasting is a core part of your membership - not an add-on.

Important details

Know before you join

Pickup only - no shipping or delivery

Wine selections are curated; individual bottle substitutions are not available

Missed pickup? Your wines will be held for you

Membership renews quarterly unless canceled before the next billing cycle

Must be 21+ to join
