How it works

Simple from start to sip

  1. Join

    Pick Nest or Songbird and sweet, dry or mixed (Songbird). Your card is saved, but nothing is charged yet.

  2. Hear what’s coming

    Before each release we email the wines, the pickup details and the charge date.

  3. Billing

    Your card is charged seven days before pickup day. Skip one release a year, or cancel before the billing deadline.

  4. Pick up & enjoy

    Collect at the gathering or during regular hours within 30 days. Bring a photo ID (21+).

Orders not picked up within 30 days are forfeited without a refund. Full membership terms are shown when you join.
